# Game of Logging

I participated in Game of Logging levels 1 and 2 at Smokey House Center last weekend. The group was great, and the instructor, Dave, shared a lot of relevant/useful/helpful info pertaining to how I currently use and plan to use the beloved Chainsaw. The beginning part of day one was quite lecture-heavy, and I think the information was a bit abstract for the course participants who were unfamiliar with the saw. Dave talked about Soren Erikson's philosophy for the course, and the biggest takeaway we'd use the in the field later that day; how his method for felling trees differs from techniques used by loggers traditionally. Day two consisted of a few demos for bucking, spring poles, and sawchain sharpening/maintenance in the classroom.
