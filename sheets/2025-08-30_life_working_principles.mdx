# Working Principles

People often ask me if I know what I want to do when I grow up. I do know exactly what I want to do, kind of. I have an idea of what kind of work I want to do, what it would take to get there, and what I would do if it didn't work out for some reaosn. I would not abide by these principes strictly. If an opportunity I have previously never considered were to present itself, I would be open to it.

I am weary of making a career out of something I enjoy in the event that doing it under financial and time-?? pressure, with monotonous repetition, would take the joy out of it. Almost feels like deja-vu.

I am good at following systems and learning new ones. I am happy to do repetitive or tedious work as long as it is in a space that feels interesting/alive, like a shop or a farm.

I have previous work experience in software, fabrication, and physical trades. I have worked in fabrication shops, in the field, and on crews. I care about doing good work, being a dependable team member, and understanding systems.
