# Pedometer Teardown

Materials used: screwdriver, hands

## Product Description

We present a pedometer with primary functions of counting and display steps, reset count, calculate/display approximate energy expenditure based on step count.

- **Weight:** 1.5 oz
- **Dimensions:** 2.4" × 2.1" × 0.98"
- **Material:** ABS plastic case, PCB with LCD display, 3 tactile switches
- **Unit retail price:** $3.22

## Key Functions

- **Detect steps, increment counter, display count**
  - Could be improved by filtering false counts.
  - Could be a useful mechanism for other products because it detects motion without using a lot of power. Can act as a switch or a counter.
- **Calculate energy expenditure based on steps.**
  - Could be improved by considering other factors such as speed, mass of user.
- **Interface with 3 buttons:** reset, set, mode.

## Solutions

- **Step detection:** spring loaded pendulum that makes contact with metal wire to complete an electrical circuit that increments count. Count is displayed by LCD that is connected to PCB using elastomeric header.
- **Energy expenditure:** To calculate energy expenditure, the step count is multiplied by some energy-cost-per-distance-unit constant.
- **Buttons:** PCB has contacts for 3 tactile button switches for functions reset, set, and mode.

## New Concepts

- Device is not accurate because it may falsely count steps when moved, or miss steps if the device is not oriented in an ideal position. Using an accelerometer may improve the accuracy.
- A tiny screwdriver is required to take apart the device. Tiny screws may be easily lost. A user may want to remove the back cover to replace the battery. One way to improve this would be to make the back cover snap-fit so it doesn't require screws.
