# Analysis of Greenhouse Heating Methods in VT Winters

## Context

Winters in Vermont are long; consequently, the outdoor growing season is short. Greenhouses are used to extend the growing season for cold-hardy crops like leafy greens but still require supplemental heat in winter. The heating methods I analyzed are electric, liquid propane, and biomass. The heating demand of a greenhouse depends on the geometry and material of the house and cover, and the outdoor temperature.

## Problem Statement

For the purpose of comparing heating methods, I'm using this 30x100' A-frame greenhouse that is 15ft tall with a double polycarbonate sheet cover. We will say that the heating system needs to keep the greenhouse at 60F. Accurate modeling of heat demand is complicated because factors like solar gain could increase the temperature inside such that the heater would not need to turn on. For simplicity, I assumed that the heater will dispatch heat when the outside temperature is below 35F and ignore solar gain. This would produce an overestimate of heat-on hours.
